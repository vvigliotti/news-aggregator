Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

SpaceHeadlines.com is a news aggregation website that provides links to publicly available reporting, government information, launch data, and federal contracting opportunities.

Information Collected Automatically

Space Headlines uses Google Analytics to understand general website traffic and usage. Google Analytics may collect information such as browser and device type, operating system, approximate geographic location, pages visited, referral source, session activity, and pseudonymous identifiers stored through cookies.

This information is used to evaluate website performance, understand which content visitors find useful, and improve the site. Space Headlines does not intentionally use Google Analytics to collect names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Space Headlines and third-party service providers may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies for traffic measurement, advertising, and related reporting. These technologies may collect or associate information about your browser, device, and activity on this website.

Google Analytics may place or access cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions and help us understand website usage. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect measurement or advertising features, but should not prevent access to the site's news links.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Space Headlines uses Google AdSense to support the website through advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve personalized advertisements based on your visits to this website and other websites on the Internet. Advertising services may also use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and device or browser identifiers to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, limit repeated advertisements, and help detect fraud.

Other third-party advertising vendors and ad networks participating in Google's advertising services may also place or read cookies and use similar technologies when advertisements are served. Their information practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Your Privacy Choices

  • Google advertising: You can manage or opt out of Google's personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Other participating advertising companies: Visit YourAdChoices to learn about participating companies and opt out of certain uses of cookies for personalized advertising.
  • Google Analytics: Google offers a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers.
  • Browser controls: You can use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Some advertising choices apply only to the browser or device where you make them and may need to be renewed after you delete cookies.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not necessarily stop advertisements from appearing; you may still receive advertisements that are not personalized.

For more information, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services, Google's Privacy Policy, and Google's information about advertising technologies.

Email Communications

If you contact Space Headlines by email, the information you provide—including your email address and message—will be used to review and respond to the communication, maintain appropriate records, protect the site, or address a reported issue.

Do not submit classified, export-controlled, proprietary, health-related, financial, or otherwise sensitive personal information.

External Links

Space Headlines links to third-party websites, including news outlets, government websites, and external data providers. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and practices. Space Headlines is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of external websites.

Data Sharing

Information may be processed by providers that support website hosting, analytics, advertising, and communications, including GitHub Pages, Google Analytics, and Google AdSense. Google and participating advertising partners may collect information directly through technologies used on this website, as described above. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect the website, or investigate misuse.

Children's Privacy

Space Headlines is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. The site does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, its features, or its service providers change. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.
